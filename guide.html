How to check if a perfume is original

Seven checks to run before and after you buy. Use them together, because no single check is enough on its own.

No single visual check can guarantee authenticity. When in doubt, purchase directly from an official brand store or authorized retailer.

1

Buy from reliable sources

The easiest way to avoid fakes is to buy where the supply chain is controlled. Brand boutiques, the brand's own website and retailers listed on the brand's store locator receive stock directly from the brand or its official distributor.

  • Start with the brand's official website or boutique.
  • Use the brand's store locator to confirm which retailers are authorised.
  • On marketplaces, look for official brand stores or official-store programmes such as Shopee Mall or LazMall, and check the exact shop name.
  • Be careful with sellers who can't explain where their stock comes from.
2

Check the packaging

Counterfeiters often cut corners on the box. Compare what you receive with photos on the brand's official website or a box you know is genuine.

  • Cellophane should be tight and neatly folded, not loose, wrinkled or covered in glue.
  • Printing should be sharp, straight and correctly spelt.
  • The cardboard should feel sturdy, and the insert should hold the bottle firmly.
  • The ingredients list, barcode and legal text should be present and easy to read.

Some genuine products, such as testers, come in plain boxes. Packaging is one clue, not proof.

3

Check batch and production information

Most genuine perfumes carry a batch code printed or embossed on the bottom of the box, with a matching code on the bottle.

  • The batch code on the box should match the code on the bottle.
  • Codes should be printed or etched cleanly, not on a sticker that peels off easily.
  • Missing or scratched-off codes are a warning sign.
  • Online batch-code checkers can estimate a production date, but they can't prove a bottle is genuine, because fakes can copy real codes.
4

Compare the bottle quality

Luxury bottles are made to a high standard. Small flaws that you would never see on a genuine bottle are common on fakes.

  • Glass should be clear and smooth, with even thickness and no bubbles or rough seams.
  • The cap should fit firmly and feel solid. Logos and engravings should be crisp.
  • The sprayer should give a fine, even mist, and the tube inside should be thin and neat.
  • The liquid colour should look like the genuine product, although it can vary slightly between batches.
5

Check the fragrance performance

Your nose is useful, but it isn't a lab test. Performance changes with skin type, weather and batch, so treat this as supporting evidence only.

  • Watch for a harsh, very alcoholic opening or an unfamiliar sour smell.
  • Notice if the scent fades much faster than a tester at an official counter.
  • If you can, compare it side by side with a tester from an official store.
6

Compare seller information

A trustworthy seller is easy to identify and easy to contact.

  • Look for a registered business (in Malaysia, SSM registration details) and a real address or contact number.
  • Read recent reviews, especially ones with photos from real buyers.
  • Check the return and refund policy before you pay.
  • Be careful if a seller avoids questions, refuses returns or asks you to pay outside the platform.
7

Be careful with unusually low prices

Real discounts exist, such as seasonal sales, gift sets and older stock. But a brand-new, sealed luxury perfume selling at a fraction of its usual price is one of the most common signs of a counterfeit.

  • Compare the price with the brand's official store.
  • Know what you are buying: testers, decants and "inspired by" dupes are not the same as a sealed original.
  • If a deal looks too good to be true, it usually is.

Quick checklist before you pay

Five questions worth asking every time.

  • Is this the brand's official store or an authorised retailer?
  • Does the price look reasonable compared with the official store?
  • Does the seller have clear business details and recent reviews?
  • Can I return the perfume if something is wrong?
  • Will I pay through the platform instead of a private transfer?

Common questions

Are testers original perfumes?

Genuine testers contain the real fragrance and are supplied to shops for customers to sample. They often come in plain boxes, sometimes without a cap. Because they aren't meant for resale, their source is hard to confirm, so only buy them from sellers you trust.

What is a decant?

A decant is a small amount of perfume moved from a full bottle into a sample vial. It's a popular way to try expensive scents, but you are trusting the seller that the original bottle was genuine.

Does a batch code checker prove a perfume is real?

No. A batch code checker can only estimate when a code says the perfume was made. Counterfeiters can copy real codes, so use it together with the other checks.

Why does the same perfume smell different on me?

Skin type, body temperature, humidity and even what you ate can change how a perfume smells and lasts. Brands also reformulate perfumes over time. A difference alone doesn't mean a bottle is fake.
