    How ScentFinder works

    From first search to a safe purchase in four steps.

    1. Search or browse

      Look up a perfume by name, brand or note, or filter by season, price and how long it lasts.

    2. Learn the scent

      Read the top, middle and base notes, the strength of the trail and the best occasions to wear it.

    3. Check the seller

      See where the perfume is sold and what each verification label means before you choose.

    4. Buy from the source

      Visit the seller's own website or store. ScentFinder never sells perfumes or takes payments.

    How to spot a fake perfume

    Counterfeits are common online, especially for popular niche scents. A few simple checks go a long way.

    No single visual check can guarantee authenticity. When in doubt, purchase directly from an official brand store or authorized retailer.

    Read the full guide

    • Buy from reliable sources

      Official brand stores and authorised retailers get stock straight from the brand or its distributor.

    • Check the packaging

      Look for tight cellophane, sharp printing and correct spelling on the box.

    • Match the batch codes

      The code on the bottom of the box should match the code on the bottle.

    • Question very low prices

      A sealed luxury perfume at a fraction of its normal price is a common warning sign.
