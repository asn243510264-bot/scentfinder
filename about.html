About ScentFinder

Why we built a perfume guide instead of another perfume shop.

ScentFinder is a perfume discovery platform created to help fragrance enthusiasts discover perfumes and identify reputable places to shop.

Buying perfume online can be confusing. The same bottle can appear at very different prices, and it is not always clear who is selling it. ScentFinder brings fragrance information and seller details together, so you can make a decision before you spend your money.

We don't sell perfumes, run a checkout or take payments. When you are ready to buy, we send you to the seller.

Mission and vision

Mission

To make perfume shopping more transparent by giving people clear fragrance information and honest guidance on where to buy.

Vision

A fragrance community in Malaysia where anyone can find their signature scent with confidence, and where counterfeit perfumes are easier to avoid.

Why ScentFinder

What makes it different from a marketplace search.

Information first

Notes, longevity, sillage, seasons and occasions are laid out clearly, so you know what a perfume smells like before you look for a price.

Honest seller labels

Sellers are only called official or verified when a reliable source confirms it. Everyone else is labelled as recommended or simply listed.

Made for beginners

The Perfume Guide explains fragrance terms and fake-spotting checks in plain language, without assuming any prior knowledge.

Local to Malaysia

Prices are shown in Ringgit, and stores are organised by Malaysian state and by the platforms people here actually use.

About the data on this site

ScentFinder is a student project. Perfume names and notes refer to real, well-known fragrances, but prices are indicative examples and bottle images are illustrations. Stores are real Malaysian sellers taken from public information; we don't show ratings because we have no reliable source for them. Always confirm stock, price and seller details before you buy.
