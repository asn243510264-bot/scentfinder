Designer, niche or luxury?

A quick guide to the three kinds of brands in the catalogue.

Designer

Made by fashion houses such as Dior or Versace. Easy to find, widely loved and usually the most affordable.

Niche

Made by houses that focus only on perfume. More unusual scents, smaller distribution and often higher prices.

Luxury

Premium lines, sometimes from fashion brands, with richer ingredients, special bottles and prices to match.
